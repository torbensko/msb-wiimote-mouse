2. The contents of this file must follow the specified format:

- wiimote-action
- windows-action

- wiimote-action = 
- 	flick-motion (WIIMOTE | NUNCHUCK) (X | Y | Z) threshold |
-       long-motion (WIIMOTE | NUNCHUCK) (X | Y | Z) threshold duration_in_milli [ONCE] |
- 	wii-key key [threshold]

flick-motion works on a difference of one polling to the next (force acceleration)
long-motion works on exceeding a threshold (force velocity)

- windows-action =
- 	key-press key1 key2 ... |
-       long-key-press duration_in_milli key1 key2 ... |
- 	mouse-click (LEFT | RIGHT | MIDDLE) (NORMAL | SINGLE_CLICK | DOUBLE_CLICK) |
- 	wheel-scroll (VERTICAL | HORIZONTAL) increments
-       recenter-ir
